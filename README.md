# Die or Die

Die Or Die is a student-ran project for CS780 at the University of New Hampshire. 
The game places you into the shoes of a kid, sick of playing the same old boardgames. 
Using only the materials available to him, he creates Die Or Die, a deck-building game 
where your life is in the hands of the odds. Build your dice bag and bring the necessary 
combinations to annihilate an evil army of Dominoes, led by the evil Don Dominos.
